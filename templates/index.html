⚠️ A Reminder About Ethical Use

Technology is a tool. How we use it is our responsibility.

Scam Alert is designed to promote online safety. Please use it responsibly and ethically.

  • Use it to protect, not deceive.
  • Respect others' privacy. Protect personal information and respect people's rights.
  • Think before you share. Avoid spreading suspicious messages or unverified information.
  • Be responsible online. Use technology to create awareness and promote digital safety.

Remember: The goal of technology should be to make the digital world safer.

Stay alert. Stay ethical. Stay responsible.
